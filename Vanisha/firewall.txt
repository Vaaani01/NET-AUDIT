- HTTPS shows self-signed SSL → strong indicator of Sophos or similar security appliance.
- Some ports (e.g., 3128, 4443) used by web interfaces / proxies / internal services — **may be allowed internally only.**
- Nmap flags show no OS banner leaked → stealth rules likely enabled.
